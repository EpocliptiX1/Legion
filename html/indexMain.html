Your all-in-one destination for both Movie and Anime lovers. This platform is based on the TMDB database while at the same time mixing in anilist and jikan for the website's anime mode, that you can toggle on the bottom left part of your screen. You can browse Anime/Movies at any time and in any mode, yet flicking it to what you are in need of will provide you better info/recommendations.
